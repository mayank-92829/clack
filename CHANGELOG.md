# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-30

First release.

- Time (15/30/60/120 s), words (10/25/50/100), quote (short/medium/long) and custom-text modes.
- Punctuation and numbers toggles; English 200 and English 1k word lists.
- Smooth caret (line, block or underline), smooth line scrolling, live WPM and accuracy, focus mode that hides the UI while typing.
- Results: WPM, accuracy, raw, consistency, character breakdown, per-second chart with errors, typed-word review.
- Personal bests per test type and a stats page with recent tests.
- 10 themes, 4 fonts, 4 font sizes, synthesized key sounds.
- Frameless window with a theme-matched title bar; per-user installer and portable exe.
